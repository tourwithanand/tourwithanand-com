Where will the driver meet me at Thiruvananthapuram Airport?

Your driver waits at the arrival area of your terminal, Terminal 1 or Terminal 2. We send you the driver's name and phone number before you land.

Is the Trivandrum Airport taxi available at night and early morning?

Yes. With advance booking we do airport pickup and drop 24 hours a day, including late-night and early-morning flights.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

Share your flight number when you book. We track your flight and your driver waits for your actual landing time.

How far is Kovalam from Trivandrum Airport?

Kovalam is about 15 km from Trivandrum Airport and the drive takes around 30 minutes in normal traffic.

How long does a taxi take from Trivandrum Airport to Varkala and Kanyakumari?

Varkala is about 45 km and takes roughly 1 hour 15 minutes. Kanyakumari is about 95 km and takes roughly 2 hours 30 minutes.

Do you offer one-way drops and outstation taxis from Trivandrum?

Yes. We do one-way drops, round trips and multi-day outstation trips from Thiruvananthapuram to Kollam, Alleppey, Kochi, Munnar, Thekkady and Kanyakumari.

Do you provide English-speaking drivers?

Yes. Our drivers speak English clearly and are experienced with international guests from Europe, the Middle East, Malaysia and Singapore.
