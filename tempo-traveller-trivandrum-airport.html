Airport group pickup and drop

Landing together at Trivandrum International Airport? One Tempo Traveller takes the whole group and the luggage from Terminal 1 or Terminal 2 to your hotel, resort or home, so nobody has to split across several cabs. For smaller groups, see our Trivandrum Airport taxi page.

Family and group tours of Kerala

Book a Tempo Traveller on rent from Trivandrum for a one-day trip to Kovalam, Poovar, Varkala or Ponmudi, or a multi-day Kerala tour through Alleppey, Thekkady, Munnar and Kochi. Kanyakumari is an easy day trip for sunrise or sunset.

Weddings and corporate travel

We move wedding guests between the airport, hotels and the venue anywhere in Thiruvananthapuram district. Companies in Technopark and Kazhakkoottam hire our minibuses for team outings, conferences and client visits.

Pilgrim groups

Tempo Travellers suit pilgrim groups visiting Sree Padmanabhaswamy Temple, Attukal, Kanyakumari and Sabarimala. Coming from Kochi instead? We also run a Sabarimala taxi from Kochi Airport.
