How your trip works

Send us your darshan slot time and we plan the pickup around it.

  1. Book on WhatsApp

    Send your travel date, pickup point, group size and darshan slot time. We confirm the vehicle and fare.

  2. Meet your driver

    Your driver waits for you at the airport arrival area or the railway station and helps with your irumudi and luggage.

  3. Travel for darshan

    Cars and the 12-seater drop you at Pamba. 17 and 26-seaters stop at Nilakkal, where KSRTC buses run to Pamba.

  4. Return to your pickup point

    After darshan, your driver brings you back to the same airport, station or city location within 24 hours.

Before you book

Plain answers on fares, timing and parking, so there are no surprises on the day.

No hidden charges

The fare shown is the fare you pay. Fuel, driver allowance, tolls and regular parking are included.

24-hour round trip

The fare covers pickup, the drive, waiting during your darshan and the return drop, all within 24 hours of pickup.

Parking at Pamba

A vehicle can stay parked at Pamba only if you hold a privilege parking pass. The pass must be arranged by you. We are not permitted to get one on your behalf.

Same fare from every pickup point

Kochi Airport, Kochi city, Aluva railway station and Ernakulam railway station all have the same fare. We drop you back where we picked you up.

Questions pilgrims ask

Not answered here? Message us on WhatsApp.

How long is the drive from Kochi Airport to Pamba?

About 4.5 to 5 hours each way, and longer on peak days. The 24-hour package is planned around the drive both ways plus your climb and darshan.

What if we need more than 24 hours?

Tell us before you travel if you expect a longer stay, for example around Makaravilakku. We will give you the extra charge before you confirm, never after the trip.

Is the fare different from Aluva or Ernakulam railway station?

No. The fare is the same from Kochi Airport, Kochi city, Aluva and Ernakulam railway stations, with drop back at the same place.

Can the vehicle wait for us at Pamba?

Only if you have your own privilege parking pass for Pamba. Otherwise the vehicle waits at the permitted parking area and your driver will tell you the pickup point, which depends on the traffic rules that day.

Do you book the darshan slot for us?

No. Please book your virtual queue slot yourself at sabarimalaonline.org and send us the slot time, so we can plan your pickup.
