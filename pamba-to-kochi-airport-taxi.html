Why the pickup is at Nilakkal, not Pamba

Many pilgrims search for a taxi from Pamba. Here is how the return trip actually works.

No taxi pickup at Pamba

Taxi pickup is not allowed at Pamba, so we cannot collect you there. Nilakkal is the base camp and parking area for Sabarimala, and that is where your car waits.

Pamba to Nilakkal by KSRTC bus

After you come down to Pamba, take the KSRTC chain service bus to Nilakkal. It is about 21 km and buses run through the season.

Book 5 to 6 hours ahead

We need at least 5 to 6 hours' notice so your vehicle can reach Nilakkal on time. Message us before you start the climb, or as soon as your darshan is over.

Tell us your flight or train time

Send your departure time when you book. We will suggest a pickup time at Nilakkal that leaves a safe margin for the drive.

How your return trip works

Four steps from Sannidhanam to your flight or train.

  1. Book on WhatsApp

    Send your pickup date and time, group size and drop point, at least 5 to 6 hours ahead. We confirm the vehicle and fare.

  2. Come down to Pamba

    Finish your darshan and walk down to Pamba. Message us when you reach, so your driver knows you are on the way.

  3. Take the bus to Nilakkal

    Board the KSRTC chain service from Pamba to Nilakkal. Your driver shares his number and the meeting point.

  4. Drive to Kochi

    Meet your driver at Nilakkal and travel directly to Kochi Airport, Ernakulam or Aluva railway station.

Where we drop you

All three drop points have the same fare.

Pamba to Kochi Airport

Drop at Cochin International Airport (COK), Nedumbassery, at the domestic or international departure terminal. Share your flight time and we plan the pickup around it.

Pamba to Ernakulam railway station

Drop at Ernakulam Junction (South) or Ernakulam Town (North). Tell us your train number so we take you to the right station.

Pamba to Aluva railway station

Aluva is the nearest major railway station to Kochi Airport and a regular stop for long-distance trains. We drop you at the station entrance.

Questions pilgrims ask

Not answered here? Message us on WhatsApp.

Can you pick us up directly from Pamba?

No. Taxi pickup is not allowed at Pamba. Please take the KSRTC chain service bus from Pamba to Nilakkal, and your driver will meet you there.

How early should I book a taxi from Nilakkal to Kochi Airport?

At least 5 to 6 hours before your pickup time. In the peak season, booking a day ahead is safer.

How long does the drive from Nilakkal to Kochi Airport take?

Usually around 4 to 5 hours, and longer on peak days. Please allow extra time if you have a flight or train to catch.

Is the fare the same to Ernakulam and Aluva railway stations?

Yes. Sedan ₹5,000, Ertiga ₹6,500 and Innova ₹7,500, whether the drop is at Kochi Airport, Ernakulam or Aluva.
