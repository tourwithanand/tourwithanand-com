Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This website, tourwithanand.com, is run by Tour With Anand (managed by Stark Holidays), Kochi, Kerala, India. This page explains what information we collect when you use the website and how we use it.

Information you send us

When you contact us on WhatsApp, by phone, by email or through a form on this website, we receive the details you choose to share, such as your name, phone number, travel dates, flight details, pickup and drop locations and number of passengers. We use these details only to reply to you, plan and provide your taxi or tour, and keep a record of your booking. We do not sell your details.

Cookies and analytics

We use the following services to understand how visitors use the website and to improve it:

  • Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager: count visits and which pages and buttons are used.
  • Google Ads: measure whether visitors who clicked our ads contacted us (for example, tapped a WhatsApp or call button).
  • Microsoft Clarity: shows us, in an anonymous way, how visitors scroll, click and move on our pages (heatmaps and session recordings), so we can find and fix parts of the site that are hard to use. Text you type into forms is hidden from these recordings. Microsoft's privacy statement: privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement.

These services use cookies or similar technology and may collect information such as your device, browser, approximate location (city or country) and the pages you visit. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time; the website will still work.

WhatsApp and other services

WhatsApp buttons open WhatsApp (owned by Meta), and their own privacy policy applies to messages you send there. Videos and maps on this site may also be served by other providers.

How long we keep information

We keep booking details only as long as needed to provide the service and for our normal business and legal records. Analytics data is kept according to the settings of the services above.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the personal details you have sent us. Contact us on WhatsApp or phone at +91 94006 20615 or by email at infostarkholidays@gmail.com.
