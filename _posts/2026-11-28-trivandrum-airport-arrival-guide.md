<h1 class="blog-title">Trivandrum Airport Arrival Guide: How to Get to Your Hotel</h1>

<p class="blog-intro"><strong>Short answer:</strong> from Trivandrum (Thiruvananthapuram) International Airport you can take a prepaid taxi from the arrivals area, book Ola or Uber, use a metered auto-rickshaw or the KSRTC electric bus, or <a href="/trivandrum-airport-taxi/" style="color:#ffcc00;">pre-book a Trivandrum Airport taxi</a>. The city centre is about 6 km away (about 20 minutes), Kovalam about 15 km (30 minutes) and Varkala about 45 km (1 hour 15 minutes).</p>

<h2 id="terminals">Check Your Terminal First</h2>
<p>Trivandrum Airport has two terminals, <strong>Terminal 1</strong> and <strong>Terminal 2</strong>. Terminal 1 is generally used for domestic flights and Terminal 2 for international flights. They have separate arrival areas, so check the terminal on your ticket or boarding pass and <strong>tell your driver or family which one it is</strong>. Do not assume they are next to each other. The airport lists a coach for transfers between the terminals if you are connecting.</p>

<h3>Prepaid taxi</h3>
<p>The airport operator lists prepaid taxi booking in the arrivals area of both terminals, and says you can pay by cash, card or a payment app. Rates can change, so ask for the current fare to your destination at the counter and keep the slip until you arrive.</p>

<h3>Ola and Uber</h3>
<p>Both are listed by the airport, with kerbside pick-up. You need a working data connection and a phone number that can receive the driver's call. If you have just landed from abroad, sort that out before you rely on an app.</p>

<h3>Bus and auto-rickshaw</h3>
<p>These are the cheapest choices. They work best in the daytime with light luggage. Check the bus timing at the airport, and make sure the auto meter is switched on before you start.</p>

<h3>Pre-booked taxi</h3>

<h2 id="late">Late-Night and Early-Morning Flights</h2>
<p>If you land late at night, decide how you will travel before you fly. Buses may be less frequent, and a long drive is easier when the driver already has your address.</p>
<ul class="blog-checklist">
<li>Save your hotel's address and phone number so you can show them offline.</li>
<li>Tell the hotel your expected arrival time, especially for homestays with a night gate.</li>
<li>Share your flight number so a delay does not cancel your pickup.</li>
<li>Keep your phone charged and your driver's number saved.</li>
</ul>
<p>Tour With Anand does airport pickup and drop 24 hours a day <strong>with advance booking</strong>. For a departure, work back from your airline's reporting time and add a margin for traffic, particularly if you are coming from Varkala, Kollam or Kanyakumari.</p>

<h2 id="where">Planning Your First Day</h2>
<h3>Beach holiday: Kovalam, Poovar or Varkala</h3>
<p>Kovalam is the closest of the three, so it is the easiest first stop after a long flight. Poovar is a little further south. Varkala is to the north and takes more than an hour. Send the exact resort name, because some beach properties are down narrow lanes or reached by boat, and your driver needs to know where the road drop is.</p>
<h3>Temple visit or city stay</h3>
<p>The city centre, East Fort and Sree Padmanabhaswamy Temple are a short drive from the airport. Temples have their own dress code and darshan timings, so check these before you set out, and allow time to leave your luggage at the hotel first.</p>
<h3>Heading further: Kanyakumari, Alleppey, Kochi or Munnar</h3>
<p>These are long drives. After an overnight flight, many travellers prefer to rest one night near the coast and start in the morning. If you are planning several days on the road, read our guide to <a href="/blog/kerala-car-with-driver-taxi-guide/">hiring a car with driver in Kerala</a>.</p>
<h3>Sabarimala pilgrims</h3>
<p>During the Mandala and Makaravilakku season, allow extra time for crowds and traffic controls. Where a vehicle may drop and pick up depends on its size: cars drop at Pamba and normally pick up at Nilakkal parking, a 12 seater Tempo Traveller drops at Pamba and picks up at Nilakkal, while 17 and 26 seaters stop at Nilakkal, where KSRTC buses run to Pamba. Rules can change on the day.</p>

<h2 id="groups">Families and Groups</h2>
<p>Choose the vehicle by passengers <em>and</em> luggage. As a guide, a sedan takes up to 3 passengers, a Maruti Ertiga up to 5 and a Toyota Innova Crysta up to 6. Large suitcases reduce comfortable capacity, so mention them when you book.</p>
<p>For bigger parties, one <a href="/tempo-traveller-trivandrum-airport/">Tempo Traveller from Trivandrum Airport</a> keeps everyone together. We offer 12, 17 and 26 seater vehicles. If your group fills every seat and carries heavy bags, ask for the next size up.</p>
