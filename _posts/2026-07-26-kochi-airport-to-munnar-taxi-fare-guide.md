<div class="blog-content">
<p>Munnar is the single most requested onward destination from Kochi International Airport, and for good reason: it's one of the most scenic drives in South India. Here's what to actually expect: distance, timing, the route itself, and how fares are calculated.</p>

<h2>Distance and Travel Time</h2>
<p>Kochi Airport to Munnar is approximately <strong>110 km</strong>, taking around <strong>3.5 hours</strong> by road under normal conditions. The route climbs steadily into the Western Ghats, so the last 90 minutes involve winding hill roads rather than highway driving. Factor in a slightly longer trip if you're prone to motion sickness and want to stop.</p>

<h2>The Route</h2>
<p>Most transfers run via Aluva and Adimali, climbing through Neriamangalam into the tea-growing hills. Along the way, common stops include Cheeyappara and Valara waterfalls, and the Nyayamakad viewpoint, all worth 10 to 15 minutes if you have flexibility in your schedule and aren't racing to check in before dark.</p>

<h2>What a Fair Fare Actually Includes</h2>
<p>A transparent quote for this route should be all-inclusive: driver allowance, tolls, and any parking, with no separate charges added on arrival. Pricing typically scales with vehicle size. A sedan or Ertiga suits couples or small families, while an Innova Crysta is the standard choice for the winding hill roads with 3 to 4 passengers and luggage, and a Tempo Traveller or Force Urbania makes sense for groups of 8 or more.</p>

<div class="blog-quote">
"Be cautious of any quote that seems unusually low. It often means tolls, waiting time, or return-fare loading get added afterward."
</div>

<p>Ask directly whether the quoted price is final before you confirm.</p>

<h2>Booking Tips</h2>
<ul>
<li>Book in advance, especially in peak season (December to February and April to May school holidays) when vehicles get scarce.</li>
<li>Share your flight number when booking so the driver can track delays and adjust pickup automatically.</li>
<li>If you want to stop at waterfalls or viewpoints en route, say so when booking. Most drivers are happy to build in stops, but it helps to set expectations upfront.</li>
</ul>
