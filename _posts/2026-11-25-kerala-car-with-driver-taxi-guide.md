<h2 id="why-driver">Why Hire a Car with Driver in Kerala?</h2>
<p>Kerala looks small on the map, but its best places are spread between the coast and the Western Ghats. Munnar and Thekkady sit high in the hills, reached by narrow, winding roads. The backwaters around Alleppey and Kumarakom are best reached door to dock. Trains do not go to the hill stations, and buses are slow with luggage.</p>
<p>A private car with a local driver solves all of this:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Door-to-door from the airport.</strong> Your driver meets you at arrivals and takes you straight to your hotel, houseboat or homestay.</li>
<li><strong>Safe hill driving.</strong> Local drivers know the hairpin bends, the fog on Munnar roads and the monsoon detours.</li>
<li><strong>Your own schedule.</strong> Stop at a waterfall, a tea estate or a spice garden when you want to, not when a bus does.</li>
<li><strong>A local guide on wheels.</strong> A good driver knows where to eat, which viewpoints are worth it, and how to avoid touts.</li>
</ul>
<p>Self-drive is possible, but most international visitors find Indian traffic, hill roads and parking stressful. With a driver, the journey becomes part of the holiday.</p>

<h2 id="fares">How Taxi Fares Work in Kerala</h2>
<p>Fares vary with the vehicle, the season and the route, so it is more useful to understand <strong>how a price is built</strong> than to compare a number you saw online.</p>
<h3>Airport transfers</h3>
<p>A one-way transfer, for example from Kochi Airport to Fort Kochi or Munnar, is usually a <strong>fixed fare</strong> agreed in advance. Our <a href="/taxi/kochi-airport/">Kochi Airport taxi service</a> works this way: one price for the trip, with tolls and airport parking included.</p>
<h3>Multi-day tours</h3>
<p>For a tour with the same car and driver over several days, the price is normally based on:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Total distance</strong> driven over the trip, often with a minimum distance per day;</li>
<li><strong>Driver allowance</strong> (sometimes called "driver bata") for each day, which covers the driver's food and overnight stay;</li>
<li><strong>Tolls, parking and state entry permits</strong>, which apply if you cross into Tamil Nadu or Karnataka;</li>
<li><strong>Night driving</strong>, which some operators charge extra for.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="itinerary">A Classic 7-Day Kerala Loop by Car</h2>
<p>This is the route most first-time visitors love: city heritage, tea hills, wildlife country and backwaters, with no long drive on any single day. Drive times between towns are approximate.</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Day 1 – Arrive at Kochi Airport, transfer to Fort Kochi (about 1.5 hours).</strong> Evening walk along the fishing nets and a Kathakali show.</li>
<li><strong>Day 2 – Fort Kochi to Munnar (about 4 hours).</strong> Stop at the Cheeyappara and Valara waterfalls on the way up into the hills.</li>
<li><strong>Day 3 – Munnar.</strong> Tea estates, the tea museum, Mattupetty Dam and Eravikulam National Park (check seasonal closures).</li>
<li><strong>Day 4 – Munnar to Thekkady (about 3 hours).</strong> Afternoon spice plantation walk.</li>
<li><strong>Day 5 – Thekkady.</strong> Morning boat ride or nature walk in Periyar Tiger Reserve.</li>
<li><strong>Day 6 – Thekkady to Alleppey (about 4 hours).</strong> Board your houseboat for a night on the backwaters.</li>
<li><strong>Day 7 – Alleppey to Kochi Airport (about 2.5 hours).</strong> Fly home, or continue south to Varkala for beach days.</li>
</ol>
