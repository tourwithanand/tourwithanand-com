Route Details & Official Parking Regulations

We typically take the traditional route via Erumeli, allowing pilgrims to stop at the Erumeli Sastha Temple for the customary Petta Thullal ritual and to offer prayers at the Vavar Mosque, embodying the spirit of religious harmony.

⚠️ Crucial Rule: Nilakkal Parking Restrictions

As per the strict guidelines issued by the Kerala Police and the Travancore Devaswom Board, no private vehicles are allowed to drive directly to Pamba.

All private taxis, traveler vans, and buses must drop off and park at the Nilakkal Base Camp. From Nilakkal, the government operates a continuous KSRTC Chain Service of AC and Non-AC buses that transport devotees the remaining 22 kilometers to Pamba.
