Route Details & Nilakkal Parking Regulations

We typically take the traditional route via Erumeli, allowing pilgrims to stop at the Erumeli Sastha Temple for the customary Petta Thullal ritual and to offer prayers at the Vavar Mosque, embodying the spirit of religious harmony.

Crucial Rule for Sabarimala Parking: As per the strict guidelines issued by the Kerala Police and the Travancore Devaswom Board, no private vehicles are allowed to drive directly to Pamba. All private taxis, traveler vans, and buses must drop off and park at the Nilakkal Base Camp. From Nilakkal, the government operates a continuous KSRTC Chain Service of AC and Non-AC buses that transport devotees the remaining 22 kilometers to Pamba.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the distance and driving time from Kochi Airport to Sabarimala?

The road distance from Kochi Airport to Nilakkal is roughly 160 km. The scenic but winding drive takes approximately 4.5 to 5.5 hours depending on seasonal traffic.

Can private taxis drive directly to Pamba?

No. Private vehicles must park at Nilakkal. Devotees then board the KSRTC chain service buses from Nilakkal to reach Pamba.

Do you offer fresh-up facilities near Kochi Airport?

Yes, our Cowboy Dorms facility is just 4 km from the airport. It's a budget-friendly AC stay perfect for up to 24 devotees to fresh-up and rest.

Can you assist with Irumudi Kettu preparation?

Yes. Our family-run service deeply understands pilgrim traditions and provides a pure, dedicated space at our dorm facility for Guruswamis to prepare the Irumudi Kettu before the yatra begins.

Do your drivers speak languages other than Malayalam?

Absolutely. Our experienced drivers speak fluent Hindi, Tamil, and English to ensure seamless communication for out-of-state devotees.

Should we pre-book our Sabarimala taxi?

Yes. During the peak Mandalam and Makaravilakku seasons (November to January), vehicle availability drops drastically. We highly recommend pre-booking your complete transport and fresh-up package well in advance.
