The Traditional Route via Erumeli

We typically follow the traditional route via Erumeli. This allows pilgrims to stop at the Erumeli Sree Dharmasastha Temple to perform the customary Petta Thullal ritual and to offer their prayers at the Vavar Mosque, beautifully embodying the spirit of religious harmony.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the distance and driving time from Kochi Airport to Sabarimala?

The road distance from Kochi Airport to the base camp is roughly 160 km. The scenic but winding drive takes approximately 4.5 to 5.5 hours depending on seasonal traffic.

Yes, our Cowboy Dorms facility is just 4 km from the airport. It's a budget-friendly AC stay perfect for up to 24 devotees to fresh-up and rest.

Can you assist with Irumudi Kettu preparation?

Yes. Our family-run service deeply understands pilgrim traditions and provides a pure, dedicated space at our dorm facility for Guruswamis to prepare the Irumudi Kettu before the yatra begins.
